Photo hide and seek

Paint a chameleon to blend into your photo, then pass the phone to a friend. They have 30 seconds to find it. Or share a picture puzzle that friends can search right in a chat.

Play offline

Practice in colorful scenes, earn stars and keep a daily challenge streak. Take a photo or choose one from your library to create your own game. No account, ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

The app interface is available in English and Russian.

Your photos stay with you

Photos and progress are stored on your device. The developer does not receive them. Delete them from the app’s settings.
